## 1. Program Structure & Economics

### 1.1 The 30% / 10% / 5% Attribution Model

- **Tier 3 (Ecosystem Network — 5%):** Third-generation liquidity flow yields 5% automated attribution.

## 2. Technical Tracking & Link Architecture

Partners can generate attribution links directly from `/[locale]/deals/[slug]` or `/[locale]/dashboard/affiliates`:

```http
https://apex-os.com/api/tracking/click?deal_id=<DEAL_UUID>&ref=<YOUR_REFERRAL_CODE>
```

### How Attribution Lock Works:
1. **30-Day Cryptographic Cookie + Subaccount Binding:** Clicking the link sets a 30-day attribution session and routes the user to bind their Binance, Bybit, OKX, or KuCoin UID/Key ID.
2. **Webhook Reconciliation:** Every trade executed on the exchange fires an HMAC-signed webhook to `/api/webhooks/exchange`.
3. **Real-Time Ledger Entry:** Our double-entry SQLite/D1 ledger credits your partner account immediately upon trade settlement.

## 4. Compliance & Brand Safety Guidelines for Partners

To protect institutional reputation and regulatory standing, all partners must adhere to the following rules:
1. **Zero Guaranteed Return Claims:** Never advertise ApexOS as an "investment scheme" or promise fixed daily/monthly ROI. Rebates are strictly proportional to executed trading fees.
2. **Accurate Security Claims:** Always emphasize that ApexOS never requests API Secret Keys, private keys, or withdrawal permissions.
3. **No Brand Bidding:** Partners may not bid on "ApexOS" branded keywords in Google Ads or impersonate official ApexOS support channels.
