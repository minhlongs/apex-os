# Site Vision

The platform unifies a 3-tier affiliate rebate network (30% / 10% / 5%) across **Binance**, **Bybit**, **OKX**, and **KuCoin**, a real-time basis-point (`bps`) cross-exchange fee arbitrage & deal crawler engine, pluggable multi-chain automated payout rails (**Stellar Horizon XLM/USDC**, **Ethereum ERC-20 USDC/USDT**, **Solana SPL USDC**) with treasury gas circuit breakers, a 30-day rolling KYC & OFAC SDN sanctions compliance engine, dual-window click fraud anomaly detection, and a viral **Telegram Mini App (TMA)** touch client (`@ApexOSBot`).
